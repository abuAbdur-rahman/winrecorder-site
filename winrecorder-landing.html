Three deliberate actions. One finished clip.

The interface keeps the recording sequence visible: choose a source, control the capture from a compact floating bar, then find the finished file in your library.

01 Choose

Pick the exact screen area

Record a display, one window, or drag a region around only the work that matters.

02 Record

Move the controls out of your way

Pause, resume, and stop from a compact bar you can park anywhere on the desktop.

03 Keep

Open the MP4 immediately

The library shows each take with its duration, size, thumbnail, and file actions.

A recorder that knows when to disappear.

WinRecorder keeps its surface small and its output ordinary. The useful part is the clip—not a proprietary project or a service you need to manage.

SRC

Display, window, or region

Choose the boundary before recording. Optional system audio and microphone stay synchronized with the picture.

MP4

No export queue

H.264 is encoded while you record, so the clip is already on disk when you press stop.

LIB

A practical local library

Reveal, rename, open, or delete a take without searching through dated folders.

OFF

Nothing running after exit

No account, telemetry, watermark, or background service. Close the app and it is closed.
